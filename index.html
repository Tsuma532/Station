Handover checklist

Handover principles

  • Use official station records for incident, case and custody details.
  • Share information only with personnel authorized to receive it.
  • Make corrections using the approved procedure; preserve audit trails.
  • Escalate unclear instructions instead of improvising outside authority.
  • Record unresolved tasks with the responsible person and next action in the official system.
This browser page does not save a handover record to a secure database.

04 / Professional standards

Conduct, accountability & safeguards

General reminders only; law, official training, current standing orders and supervisors take precedence.

⚖️ Lawful, proportionate action

Act within legal authority, respect rights and dignity, and seek supervisory or legal guidance where required.

🔒 Information protection

Keep personal data, statements, case files, intelligence and internal station records within approved systems and authorized access.

📝 Accurate records

Ensure records are timely, factual, attributable and corrected only through approved procedures.

🤝 Public service

Communicate respectfully, explain available public services and refer complaints or vulnerable people appropriately.

🧑‍⚕️ Welfare & safeguarding

Follow current rules for custody welfare, medical needs, vulnerable persons and prompt escalation of risks.

📦 Property accountability

Use authorized registers and chain-of-custody procedures for property, exhibits and controlled items.

05 / Scope & references

About Station Desk

What this prototype does

  • Provides a general daily-duty checklist and shift-handover aid.
  • Indexes common station record categories without reproducing confidential operational records.
  • Links to public National Police Service form and resource pages.
  • Works as a standalone HTML demonstration without a database.

What it does not do

  • It is not an official NPS product or endorsed by the National Police Service.
  • It does not create, store, or authenticate real officer accounts.
  • It does not replace official books, registers, case systems, standing orders or training.
  • It is not a complete list of every register used in every Kenyan station.
